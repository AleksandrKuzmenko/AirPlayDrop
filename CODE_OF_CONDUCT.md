# Code of Conduct

AirPlayDrop contributors and participants must be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in repository discussions, code review, or related community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/AleksandrKuzmenko/APremote/security/advisories/new). Include relevant links or screenshots and state that the report concerns conduct rather than a software vulnerability. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible; details will be shared only when needed to investigate or respond.
